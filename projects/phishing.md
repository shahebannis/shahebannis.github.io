# Phishing Detector Web Application

This web application is designed to educate users about phishing emails and provide robust protection against online scams. Through a comprehensive suite of resources, users can learn about the deceptive tactics employed by cybercriminals, recognize suspicious email patterns, and understand common phishing techniques. The platform offers practical tips and best practices to empower individuals to safeguard their personal and financial information online. Our expert guidance ensures users remain informed, vigilant, and secure in the digital landscape.

## Machine Learning-Based Spam Detection

At the core of the application is a logistic regression machine learning model, trained on over 10,000 data records using Python's `scikit-learn` and `joblib` libraries. This model achieves a high accuracy rate of 94% in predicting whether a given email is spam or legitimate. Users can leverage this predictive capability to automatically assess the safety of incoming emails, enhancing their protection against phishing attempts.

## Cybersecurity Training & Educational Features

The application is also tailored for organizations seeking to provide cybersecurity training to their employees. Key educational features include:

- **User Accounts:** Employees can create accounts and securely log in to the platform.
- **Interactive Quizzes:** Users can take quizzes to evaluate their knowledge of spam and phishing emails. Each quiz presents sample emails from a pool of approximately 5,000 examples, challenging users to identify spam.
- **Performance Tracking:** The number of quizzes attempted and individual results are tracked for each user. Administrators (employers) have access to these metrics, enabling them to monitor employee progress and identify areas for improvement.
