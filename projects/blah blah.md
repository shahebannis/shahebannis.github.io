IoT:
This project showcases the potential of IoT solutions to create smart, responsive environments that prioritize user comfort, security, and convenience through automation and seamless

Using an mqtt server to connect our smart system to our custom built website, we created a smart system similar to one found in a smart home.

it has a light, controllable through the website and automatically turns on whenever the ambient light level gets too low.
Humidity and temperature sensors
keycard scanner with proper authentication to remember your preferred settings
We had motors that were controllable through the website that would act as a fan. It would also turn on automatically after the temperature gets too hot
We also had email functionalities.
We also had an automated system that would notify you by email if the temperature is too low and would turn it back on when you reply yes to the original email.
Whenever a new person would log in, a notification email would be send.
Same with light intensity
